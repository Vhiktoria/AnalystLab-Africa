# ShopSmart Retail Analytics Using Microsoft Excel

## Project Overview

ShopSmart Retail Group has data covering its sales, customers, products, stores, returns, and inventory. Analysing these areas together can help the business understand its performance, identify issues, and make better operational decisions.

In this project, I analysed the provided datasets using Microsoft Excel to examine sales trends, compare store and product category performance, review customer activity, and identify potential inventory and product return concerns. I then brought the findings together in an interactive dashboard and developed recommendations based on the results.

## Business Objectives

- Evaluate overall sales performance.
- Compare revenue across product categories, stores, and regions.
- Identify products and customers with high recorded sales.
- Examine monthly sales trends.
- Review product returns and identify items that may need further investigation.
- Identify inventory records at or below their reorder levels.
- Present the findings in a dashboard to support business decision-making.

## Dataset

- **Business:** ShopSmart Retail Group
- **Data Provided:** Nine datasets covering customers, products, orders, stores, returns, employees, suppliers, inventory, and order items.
- **Supporting File:** Data Dictionary
- **Tool Used:** Microsoft Excel

## Data Preparation and Analysis

I reviewed the datasets for duplicate records and missing values, examined key fields and relationships, and used Excel formulas and lookups to bring relevant information together. I then created calculated fields and PivotTables to analyse sales, returns, and inventory.

The results were presented through charts, KPI cards, and interactive filters in an Excel dashboard.

## Key Insights

- Beauty generated the highest revenue among the product categories, at approximately ₦1.76 billion.
- ShopSmart Lagos recorded the highest revenue among the listed stores, at approximately ₦1.23 billion.
- December recorded the highest monthly sales, at approximately ₦660.63 million.
- The South region generated the highest regional revenue, at approximately ₦2.42 billion.
- Home Item 263 had the highest recorded return count, with 11 returns.
- Fourteen stores had inventory records at or below their reorder levels, highlighting areas that may need restocking attention.

## Business Recommendations

- Review the profitability and stock availability of products in the Beauty category before making further investment decisions.
- Prioritise inventory checks and replenishment for stores with low-stock records.
- Investigate the reasons for returns involving Home Item 263 and other frequently returned products.
- Compare store and regional performance to identify practices that could be applied across other locations.
- Monitor monthly sales trends to improve inventory planning and sales activities.

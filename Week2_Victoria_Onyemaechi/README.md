
# Global Superstore Sales & Profitability Dashboard | Power BI

## Project Overview

This project involved analysing the Global Superstore dataset using Microsoft Power BI to understand sales performance, profitability, customer segments, product categories, regional performance, and monthly sales trends.

The aim was to turn raw sales data into an interactive dashboard that makes it easier to identify business performance patterns, highlight areas that need attention, and support data-driven decision-making.

## Data Preparation

The dataset contained 51,290 rows and 24 columns. Before developing the dashboard, I reviewed the data and carried out data quality checks.

My preparation process included:

- Checking for duplicate records and identical rows. No duplicates were identified.
- Reviewing missing values. Postal Code had missing values in approximately 81% of records, so I retained the column rather than introducing unsupported values.
- Reviewing and correcting data types where necessary.
- Retaining negative profit values because they represent losses and are important to profitability analysis.
- Checking discount values against the expected range of 0 to 1. No values outside this range were identified.

I then used the prepared dataset to create measures and develop the report in Power BI.

## Key Performance Indicators

I created five DAX measures to provide an overview of business performance.

| Metric | Result |
|---|---:|
| Total Sales | 12.64M |

| Average Sales per Order | 504.99 |
| Profit Margin | 11.61% |

### DAX Measures

**Total Sales**
```dax
Total Sales = SUM('Global Superstore'[Sales])
```

**Total Profit**
```dax
Total Profit = SUM('Global Superstore'[Profit])
```

**Total Orders**
```dax
Total Orders = DISTINCTCOUNT('Global Superstore'[Order ID])
```

**Average Sales per Order**
```dax
Average Sales = DIVIDE([Total Sales], [Total Orders])
```

**Profit Margin**
```dax
Profit Margin = DIVIDE([Total Profit], [Total Sales], 0)
```

## Dashboard Features

The dashboard contains nine visuals and two interactive slicers.

- Sales by Region: compares sales contributions across regions.
- Profit by Region: highlights differences in regional profitability.
- Sales by Customer Segment: shows the distribution of sales across Consumer, Corporate, and Home Office customers.
- Sales by Category: compares sales across Furniture, Office Supplies, and Technology.
- Profit by Category: compares the profit generated by each category.
- Monthly Sales Trend: examines monthly sales patterns across 2011–2014.
- Top 10 Products by Profit: identifies the ten products with the highest profit.
- Regional Sales and Profit Matrix: compares performance across regions and product categories.
- Profit Margin by Category: compares the profitability of the three product categories.
- Year and Region Slicers: allow users to filter the report and explore the data interactively.

## Key Findings

**1. Technology generated the highest category profit.**

Technology recorded approximately 4.74M in sales and 663.78K in profit, with a profit margin of 13.99%. It had the highest profit contribution among the three categories.

**2. Consumer customers contributed the largest share of sales.**

The Consumer segment accounted for 51.48% of sales, followed by Corporate at 30.25% and Home Office at 18.27%.

**3. Central was the leading region by sales and profit.**

Central recorded approximately 2.82M in sales and 311.40K in profit, making it the strongest region by both measures in the report.

**4. Monthly sales generally increased across the years analysed.**

January sales rose from approximately 98,898.49 in 2011 to 241,268.56 in 2014. November 2014 recorded approximately 555,279.03 in sales, the highest monthly value among the figures reviewed.

**5. Furniture had the lowest profit margin.**

Furniture generated approximately 4.11M in sales and 285.20K in profit, but its profit margin was 6.94%, compared with 13.99% for Technology and 13.69% for Office Supplies.

**6. Southeast Asia requires closer profitability analysis.**

The region recorded approximately 0.88M in sales but only 17.85K in profit. Its Furniture category also recorded a loss of approximately 7,269.77 despite generating sales. This makes the region and category combination an important area for further investigation.

**7. The top-performing products included copiers and smartphones.**

The Canon imageCLASS 2200 Advanced Copier had the highest profit among the top ten products displayed, at approximately 25,199.93. Several smartphone products also appeared in the ranking.

## Business Recommendations

Based on the findings, I recommend:

- Reviewing Furniture pricing, discounts, and costs to understand its lower profit margin.
- Investigating the transactions responsible for losses in Southeast Asia Furniture.
- Examining top-performing Technology products to identify opportunities for further growth.
- Exploring ways to increase sales from Corporate and Home Office customers.
- Comparing regional product mix and profitability to identify areas requiring attention.
- Using monthly sales patterns to support inventory and operational planning.

These recommendations identify areas for action and further analysis; the dashboard does not establish the underlying causes of every performance difference.
